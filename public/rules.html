Games Rules

Concept :

In ANOMALY, dive into the dynamics of workplace soft skills while detecting deception and outsmarting your friends.

Use wit, observation, and strategy to uncover impostors—or fool everyone if you're the Anomaly.

Objective :

Employers: Collaborate to expose the Anomaly and the Glitch(es) while protecting the secret word.

Glitch: Blend in by defending your similar-but-different word. Your goal is to confuse and mislead the Employers.

Anomaly: Without knowing the secret word, decipher the Employers' clues while staying undetected. Guess the secret word if eliminated for an instant win.

Setup :

4–6 players : 1 Anomaly, 1 Glitch, and the rest are Employers.

7–10 players : 1 Anomaly, 2 Glitches, and the rest are Employers.

Distribute cards face-down. Roles and words remain secret. Word Selection: The Employers and Glitches are given a single word (the Glitches receive a slightly altered version). The Anomaly receives no word.

How to play :

  1. Starting the Game

    The player with the "Beginner" card starts the first round.

  2. Gameplay: Word Contribution

    Players take turns (clockwise) contributing a word:

    • Employers: Say a word related to the secret word on their card.
    • Glitches: Defend their alternate word as if it were the secret word.
    • Anomaly: Create plausible responses in line with the overall theme, aiming to deduce the secret word.
  3. Word Rules

    No Repetitions: Each word must be unique.
    No Synonyms or Word Families: Avoid overly obvious connections to the secret word.
    Anomaly's Role: Use creativity and deduction to blend in while collecting clues.

  4. Discussions & Voting

    After each round, players discuss their suspicions and vote:
    Step 1: Vote to identify the Anomaly.
    Step 2: If the Anomaly is eliminated, vote to identify the Glitches.

  5. Role Reveal

    The player with the most votes reveals their card:
    If an Employer: The team loses a valuable ally.
    If a Glitch: The team moves closer to victory.
    If the Anomaly: The player gets one chance to guess the secret word.

Winning Conditions :

Employers : Successfully eliminate the Anomaly and all Glitches.

Glitches : Survive undetected or cause enough confusion to disrupt the Employers' strategy.

Anomaly : Stay undetected until the end of the game. OR, if eliminated, correctly guess the secret word for an immediate win.

Strategic Tips :

Employers : alance clarity and subtlety when giving clues. Avoid being too obvious or too vague. Observe others closely and listen for inconsistencies.

Glitches : Defend your alternate word convincingly. Use plausible associations without overreaching.

Anomaly : Pay attention to patterns in players' words. Avoid hesitation or overconfidence—blend in naturally.
