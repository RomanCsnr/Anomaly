About The Game

Anomaly is a serious game designed to improve players' English skills while exploring professional soft skills. Focused on workplace adaptability, communication, and critical thinking, the game encourages players to expand their vocabulary and enhance their strategic thinking.
Whether in digital or physical format, Anomaly provides a fun and challenging way to build essential skills while fostering collaboration and learning in a professional context.

Roles

Collaborate to expose the Anomaly and the Glitch(es) while protecting the secret word.

Blend in by defending your similar but different word. Your goal is to confuse and mislead the Employers.

Without knowing the secret word, decipher the Employers’ clues while staying undetected. Guess the secret word if eliminated for an instant win.

Rules

Distribute cards face-down. Roles and words remain secret. Word Selection : The Employers and Glitches are given a single word (the Glitches receive a slightly altered version). The Anomaly receives no word.
